Jabro Draw - test version
=========================

1. Double-click "Jabro-Draw-0.1.0-Setup.exe".

2. Windows will probably say "Windows protected your PC", because the program is
   new and not signed with a paid certificate. Click "More info", then
   "Run anyway".

3. Click "Install". It installs just for you (no administrator password needed)
   and puts Jabro Draw in the Start menu and on the desktop.

To remove it later: Settings > Apps > Installed apps > Jabro Draw > Uninstall.
Your drawings are never touched by installing or removing it.

Opening HEIC (iPhone) photos needs the "HEIF Image Extensions" (free) and the
"HEVC Video Extensions" (a small fee, or preinstalled by many PC makers) from
the Microsoft Store. AVIF needs the free "AV1 Video Extension". Many Windows 11
computers already have them.

Everything, including the AI tools, runs on your own computer. Pictures are
never uploaded anywhere.
